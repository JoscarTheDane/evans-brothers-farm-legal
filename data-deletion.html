User Data Deletion

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This page explains how to have the personal information we hold about you deleted, and what happens when you ask.

1. How to ask

Send a deletion request to either of these, and use the subject line "Delete my data":

  • Email: jsh.evan@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: +27 76 559 4033 — message us: "Please delete my data"

Please tell us the name and phone number (or Facebook profile name) you used when you contacted us, so we can be sure we delete the right records.

2. What we delete

  • your message history with us on WhatsApp;
  • your enquiry and contact details in our records;
  • any notes we made about your enquiry.

3. What we may have to keep

We may keep a limited record where the law requires it (for example, accounting or tax records), or where we need it to resolve an ongoing dispute. We will tell you if that applies, and we delete it as soon as we are permitted to.

4. What we cannot delete

Messages you sent through Facebook or WhatsApp also sit with Meta Platforms, on your own account. We cannot delete those for you — you control them from your own Facebook or WhatsApp settings. Deleting our copy does not remove Meta's copy.

5. How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days, and we will confirm to you when it is done.

6. Questions

If anything here is unclear, email jsh.evan@gmail.com and ask. Our Privacy Policy explains more about how we handle personal information.
