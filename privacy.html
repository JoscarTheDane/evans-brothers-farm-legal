Privacy Policy

Evans Brothers' Farm — Curry's Post, Howick, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you interact with Evans Brothers' Farm through our Facebook Page or our WhatsApp assistant, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. We handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

Evans Brothers' Farm is a working farm near Curry's Post, Howick, in the KwaZulu-Natal midlands. We are the responsible party for the personal information described here.

2. What we collect

When you message our Facebook Page or our WhatsApp number, we may receive:

  • your name and the display name on your Facebook or WhatsApp profile;
  • your phone number (WhatsApp) or Page-scoped identifier (Facebook);
  • the content of the messages you send us, including any photos or documents;
  • basic technical information the platform provides, such as timestamps.

We do not ask for, and do not want, special personal information (for example health or religious information) or your financial details. Please do not send these.

3. How we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and answer your questions;
  • to arrange and administer bookings, visits, livery, lessons or other services;
  • to keep a record of our communication with you;
  • to improve how we run the farm and the services we offer.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Automated replies

Some replies from our WhatsApp number are generated with the help of an automated (AI) assistant, which drafts responses on our behalf. A person from the farm remains responsible for the service you receive. If you would prefer to speak to a person, just say so, and we will pick it up directly.

5. Who we share it with

Our Page and our WhatsApp number both run on Meta Platforms' services, so your message necessarily passes through Meta (Facebook / WhatsApp) and is subject to Meta's own privacy terms. Beyond that, we only share personal information with service providers who help us run the farm, and only where they need it — or where the law requires us to.

6. How long we keep it

We keep message records only for as long as we need them to help you and to run our business sensibly, and then we delete them. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so (see section 8 below).

7. Children

Some of our services involve children. Where a child is under 18, we expect an adult parent or guardian to handle the enquiry and to consent on their behalf.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to:

  • confirm what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete information we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.

You can also object to how we are handling your information, and you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. Instructions for deletion are set out on our Data Deletion page.

9. How we protect it

We keep access to our Page and WhatsApp accounts limited to the people who run the farm, and we use industry-standard security on the devices and accounts we control. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously.
